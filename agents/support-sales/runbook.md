# Runbook — support-sales agent

- **Document answers fall back to "no information" too often:** read the turn trace (`check_reasons`). `quote_not_in_source` and `unsupported_claim` mean a sentence was not backed by its passage; `number_not_in_quote` and `price_in_answer` mean a number or price did not come from the passage; `link_not_in_quote` means a sentence carried a web address, email address or markdown link that its quote does not contain; `clarify_not_offered` means the model asked a clarifying question, which the shop's prompt never offers; an empty source list means retrieval found nothing — check the active knowledge version and the retrieval evaluation.
- **Wrong or outdated facts:** opening hours and contacts are written from approved records: fix the business pack, then draft, review, approve and activate a new version, or roll back with `frontdesk knowledge rollback --env-file deploy/<business>.env`. The status of an order comes from the shop's order data (the pack's `orders` file today), not from a knowledge version: correct that data and restart the deployment.
- **Classifier rejections spike:** read the `reason` field in the logs (`intent_not_allowed`, `text_has_clinical_terms`, …). The shop has no services or doctors to add, so look at the classifier prompt version in the traces and at the offline evaluation.
- **The deployment refuses to start after an embedder change:** run `frontdesk knowledge reembed --env-file deploy/<business>.env`.
- **Stop the bot:** until the kill switch exists (M5), stop the process; conversations stay visible to shop staff on Instagram.
